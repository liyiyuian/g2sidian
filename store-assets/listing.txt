APP NAME
--------
Gbsidian


SUBTITLE / TAGLINE
------------------
Obsidian reader, editor on your glasses


SHORT DESCRIPTION
-----------------
View and voice-capture your Obsidian notes on Even G2 glasses, hands-free. A tiny
backend runs on your own computer, reads your vault files directly, and is reachable
only over your own Tailscale network (token-required, loopback-bound). No central
server, no accounts, no analytics — your notes never leave your machine.


DESCRIPTION
-----------
Gbsidian puts your Obsidian notes where you can just glance at them. Pick a vault once on your phone and your glasses open straight into it — no phone in your hand.

What you can do, hands-free, on the glasses:
• Jump in: open today's daily note, recently modified notes, your Bookmarks, or what's due across the vault — in two taps.
• Glance: your Obsidian Tasks and Dataview (LIST/TASK) query blocks render as live results. Common task dashboards built with dataviewjs render too; other dataviewjs blocks show a placeholder.
• Browse and read: move through your folder tree and read notes rendered to clean lines (wikilinks, callouts, tasks, tables, headings, embedded notes). Tap to follow a [[link]] or jump to a heading; double-tap walks back.
• Search: find a note by full text, filename, alias, or #tag.
• Capture by voice: speak a thought into today's daily note, an inbox, or the note you're reading — swipe to pick where it goes, check the preview, save. Or just type on the phone instead.

Gestures: tap = open, double-tap = back, swipe = move/scroll. Tap then hold opens a menu: Quick capture, Search this vault, or Vault home — from any screen.

Private by design: a small backend on YOUR own computer reads your vault files directly — loopback-bound, token-required, reachable only over your own Tailscale network. No central server, no accounts, no analytics; the developer collects nothing and your notes never leave your machine.

Voice is optional: it needs an OpenAI Whisper key on your backend, and dictated audio then goes to OpenAI for transcription. Without a key, you type on the phone and nothing leaves your own backend.

Requires the Even Realities app 2.2.10 or later and a one-time, self-hosted backend setup — clone https://github.com/liyiyuian/g2sidian and run ./install.sh (needs your Obsidian vault folder + Tailscale). Gbsidian is an independent app: not affiliated with Obsidian, no Obsidian plugin required, no cloud sync, no multi-user server.

PERMISSIONS (justifications)
----------------------------
network — Connects the glasses app to your own self-hosted backend over your private
Tailscale network to read your Obsidian vault, run searches and queries, and save captured
notes. The app talks only to the backend URL you enter in Setup; no other servers are
contacted, except OpenAI for transcription if you enable voice.

g2-microphone — Captures your voice for hands-free quick-capture into a daily note or inbox
and for appending dictation to the note you're reading. Used only when you start a voice
capture; if you leave voice disabled, no microphone audio is used and you type on the phone.
